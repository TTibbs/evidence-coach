# Evidence Coach Product Audit

Last updated: 2026-07-31

## Summary

Evidence Coach has a strong MVP spine: verified evidence is the source of truth, and CV generation, job matching, and interview practice all reuse confirmed cards. The biggest product opportunity is to make those surfaces feel like one guided preparation system for a specific role or interview, rather than separate tools.

## Most Important Gap

The app should own the role-specific preparation loop:

1. User pastes or selects a job description.
2. App identifies role requirements.
3. App maps confirmed evidence to those requirements.
4. App turns gaps into evidence-gathering prompts.
5. App generates realistic practice questions.
6. App helps the user retry answers and package the result.

This is the path from “useful MVP” to “I would pay because my interview is soon.”

## Killer Feature Bet: Interview Prep Pack

Create an Interview Prep Pack for a saved job target.

The pack should include:

- Requirements matrix from the job description.
- Best confirmed evidence for each requirement.
- Missing-evidence prompts for uncovered requirements.
- Likely interview questions grounded in the role and evidence gaps.
- Suggested answer outlines using confirmed evidence only.
- Practice queue and retry progress.
- Exportable one-page preparation summary.

Why this complements the app:

- It uses the existing evidence bank, job targets, builder, and practice features.
- It supports the PRD's Interview Pass plan.
- It gives urgent users a clear customer-winning outcome: “I know what examples I will use in this interview.”

## Highest-Impact Improvements

1. Generate practice questions from selected evidence cards and job targets.
2. Add “Build evidence for this gap” actions from job target gaps.
3. Add “Practise this job” from job target detail pages.
4. Create a first Prep Pack page that aggregates matched evidence, gaps, likely questions, and practice links.
5. Show “facts used” beside generated CV/interview content so users trust the output.

## Important MVP Gaps

- Practice setup still requires manual question entry.
- Evidence gaps are visible but not always action-oriented.
- Job target analysis does not yet become a guided preparation workflow.
- Generated output history is shallow compared with a true revision trail.
- Claim provenance should be more visible in generated outputs.

## Later Bets

- Browser extension for job descriptions.
- Job-board imports.
- Full CV document editor.
- OAuth sign-in.
- Offline voice practice.

These are useful, but they should follow the role-specific preparation loop rather than distract from it.
